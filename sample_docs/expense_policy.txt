Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable business expenses incurred while
performing their duties. Submit expenses through the finance portal within 30
days of the expense date.

Receipts are required for any expense over 25 USD. Meals during business travel
are reimbursed up to a daily limit of 75 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current published rate.
International travel must be approved by a director in advance.

Reimbursements are paid out with the next payroll cycle after approval, which
typically means funds arrive within two weeks of submission.
