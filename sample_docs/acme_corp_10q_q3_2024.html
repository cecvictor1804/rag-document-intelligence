ACME CORP — FORM 10-Q

Quarterly report pursuant to Section 13 or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 for the quarterly period ended September 30, 2024.

Management's Discussion and Analysis

Net sales increased 6.1% year over year, driven by continued growth in the Widgets Pro segment and higher attach rates on service plans. Gross margin expanded on favorable component pricing, partially offset by freight. Operating expenses grew slower than revenue as hiring normalized. We expect seasonal strength in the December quarter, though foreign exchange remains a headwind to international revenue.
