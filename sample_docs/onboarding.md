# Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the company. This guide covers your first week.

## Getting your accounts

On day one, IT provisions your Google Workspace account. You will receive an
email with a temporary password. Sign in at the company portal and you will be
prompted to enable two-factor authentication.

## Resetting your password

If you forget your password, go to the company portal and click "Forgot
password". A reset link is sent to your recovery email. Reset links expire after
30 minutes. If your account is locked after five failed attempts, contact the IT
help desk to unlock it.

## Requesting software

Standard software (browser, editor, chat) is pre-installed. To request
additional licensed software, file a ticket in the IT service desk and include
your manager's approval.
